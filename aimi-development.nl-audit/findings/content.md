# Content Quality & E-E-A-T — 2026-09-04

Score: **76/100**

## What works

- **No thin content.** Site average 980 words. City pages average 1,051; branch pages average 1,177. Nothing on the site is a stub.
- **Programmatic pages are genuinely differentiated** — the hardest thing to get right at this scale:
  - City pages: **7.7% H2 overlap**. Headings are written per city, not templated. Veendam gets *"Waarom een webdesigner uit Veendam zelf verschil maakt"*; Hoogeveen gets *"Online opvallen in een stad met een streekfunctie"*. Different angles, not find-and-replace.
  - Branch pages: 23.6% H2 overlap — higher but still legitimate, since a shared spine ("Wat een website moet kunnen" / "Wat kost het") is appropriate for a comparable-services set.
  - **249 unique FAQ questions** across 36 pages; only 29 reused, and the most-reused ("Wat kost een website laten maken?") appears just 5 times. This is real writing, not spun text.
- **No duplicate titles or descriptions** anywhere on 48 pages.
- Honest positioning: the SEO page explicitly says *"Geen beloftes over posities"* — a credibility signal Google's quality raters reward.

## Findings

### CQ-1 — No KvK or BTW number anywhere on the site (High)
Searched all 48 pages: **0 hits for KvK, 0 for BTW**. For a Dutch business this is both:
- **A legal problem.** The Handelsregisterwet and EU e-commerce rules require a Dutch business to state its KvK number and VAT ID on its website.
- **An E-E-A-T problem.** These are the cheapest, hardest-to-fake trust signals available, and competitors will have them.

The contact page shows only an email address and a phone number — no company registration details and no postal address.

### CQ-2 — Weak authorship and experience signals (High)
"Aidan" and "Milan" appear on only **6 of 48 pages** (`/`, `/werkwijze`, `/over-ons`, `/contact`, and the two legal pages). The 30 programmatic city/branch pages — the pages actually built to rank — carry no named human, no bio, no credential, no photo.

For Google's *Experience* and *Expertise* criteria this is the site's biggest content gap: 1,000+ words of solid advice attributed to nobody.

### CQ-3 — Almost no social proof (High)
Review/testimonial language appears on **1 of 48 pages**. There is no `aggregateRating`, no named client, no case study, no portfolio piece with a result. For a web agency — where prospects buy on demonstrated craft — this is the weakest link in the funnel, and it is what "Ook interessant" blocks currently link past.

### CQ-4 — Zero external links (Medium)
The site links out to **nothing**. Not to web.dev on Core Web Vitals, not to Google's documentation, not to KvK, not to a single source.

Outbound citation to authoritative sources is a recognised quality signal and matters increasingly for AI citation — engines favour content that situates itself in a wider, verifiable context. A site that cites nothing reads as a closed brochure.

### CQ-5 — `/contact` is comparatively thin (Low)
304 words, the shortest page on the site. Acceptable for a contact page, but it is missing the details that make one useful and locally relevant: postal address, opening hours, response-time commitment, service area, route description.

### CQ-6 — No fresh-content surface (Low)
No blog, no changelog, no dated articles. Only one page carries `Article` schema (`/wordpress-of-maatwerk`). Every URL is an evergreen service page. This caps topical breadth, gives no natural link-earning asset, and offers Google no freshness signal.
